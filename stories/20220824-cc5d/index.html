01. 项目概览Program Introduction

中国·新加坡 全球青年领袖峰会是CNEW国际旗下的一个非盈利性质的社会影响项目，我们已经培训并赋能了来自15个国家的10,000多名青年领袖。

The Global Youth Leadership Program is a non-profit social impact project under CNEW International, we have trained and equipped over 10,000 young leaders from 15 countries.


峰会为18岁-30岁的未来领导人所设计，致力于培养必要的技能和洞察力，帮助他们更好地领导自己和团队，以及提高如你一般的变革者与领导者的能力，以便在这个世界上产生影响，改善人类的福祉。

The Global Youth Leadership Program is designed for future leaders from age 18 years old to 30 years, this program builds the necessary skills and insights to help them better lead themselves and their teams. As well as to empower change makers and leaders like you to make an impact in this world and improve the well-being of humanity.

Together, 

we are delighted to bring you our 2.5 days 

8th GYLP on 26th, 27th and 28th August 2022.


02. 项目愿景 Program Goals

GYLP 2022为学员提供必要的技能、思维方式和洞察力，以便在一个加速颠覆性变化的世界中，保持对事物的根本关注，并成为一个更好的领导者。该课程为准备进一步提高领导力的高潜力未来领导人而设计，注重通过小组讨论进行体验式学习，并鼓励参与者进行深入的沟通交流，以便他们能够在日常生活和工作中重现和应用所学。

GYLP 2022 provides participants with the necessary skills, ways of thinking, and insights to become a better leader in a world of accelerating disruptive change, through crisis, by maintaining a radical focus on what is within their control. Designed for high potential future leaders who are ready to further their leadership skills, the program focuses on experiential learning with group discussions, and empowers participants to interact with each other so they can reproduce and apply the learning in their daily life and work.
