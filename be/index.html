About us

AWDPI's work began in Belgium, where the Avoice programme was launched in 2020 to support overseas Chinese women affected by domestic violence. AWDPI Belgium supports Asian women living in Belgium who face domestic violence, gender-based violence or discrimination, with multilingual help, legal information, psychological support and referrals to local services. It also works with local organisations and volunteers to raise awareness of gender-based violence and to build community support for Asian women.

Our place in the AWDPI network

AWDPI Belgium is an independent international non-profit association (AISBL) registered in Belgium. It is part of the AWDPI international network, together with AWDPI United Kingdom, AWDPI United States and AWDPI Australia. Each organisation is registered separately and is responsible for its own activities, funds and personal data.
